# Caveman

Apply lite compression in every task to internal AI communication only. Reduce
unnecessary input first: exact scope, bounded output, relevant files, retained
receipts and no repeated unchanged checks. Prefer counts or selected scalar rows
to dumping whole dirty trees, registries or skill catalogs.

Never lose negation, numbers, units, exact symbols, commands, dates, uncertainty,
authority or safety meaning. Do not invent abbreviations or impair grammar.
Required progress updates remain mandatory. Use normal detail when compression
would obscure ambiguity, security, destructive actions or operation ordering.

User-facing replies, documentation, code, comments, commits, issues and reports
remain normal clear prose. No upstream plugin, hook, runtime, installer, external
model or subagent is required. Trading/capture and verification rules do not change.
