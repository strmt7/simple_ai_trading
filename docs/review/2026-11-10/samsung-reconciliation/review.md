# Samsung dividend true-up follows the earlier position

The retained Binance notices and Samsung disclosure reconcile exactly.
Binance announced an estimate of **4,500 KRW per share** and a later true-up
of **101 KRW per share**. Together they match the issuer's **4,601 KRW**
common-share dividend. The publisher records were retained and checked against
their native receipts. [Estimate notice](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/fd194d0fda6e44f2af6bc6333f61600f) ·
[True-up notice](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/c88b113255244de1953dd416c4806bc3) ·
[Issuer disclosure](https://www.samsung.com/global/ir/reports-disclosures/public-disclosure-view.84796/)

Binance tied both components to the net position at **September 28, 23:00
UTC**. The scheduled true-up started **November 6, 06:00 UTC**, 919 hours
later. Samsung separately approved the amount on October 29 and listed
November 27 as its scheduled shareholder payment date. The Binance estimate
notice excludes the adjustment from ordinary funding caps, and the later
notice directs users to the Special Funding Fee account record.

The difference matters even after a position closes. Ten share units at the
September snapshot still imply a nominal **1,010 KRW** adjustment magnitude
under the announced rule if the position is flat in November. Ten units
opened only after the snapshot receive no allocation from that event. A replay
needs the earlier position for entitlement and the later posting for usable
cash and margin. It also needs a separate source availability time so the
final issuer amount cannot leak into a September decision.

The exact derivation verifies those terms and the dates offline. Actual wallet
sign, settlement currency, FX conversion, cash availability and the Polymarket
counterpart's treatment remain unqualified. The CMS fields do not identify the
exact edit time of the revised amount. This retrospective source reconciliation
adds no market observation and does not reopen a funding or order-book window.
