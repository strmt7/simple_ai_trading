# Maker income: a new methodological source, not a neutral edge

The September 17 [author-submitted preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20017v1)
by Zang, Andrade and Nakajima was absent from retained title/identifier/author
aliases. Two frozen, bounded public GETs retained its abstract and the exact
versioned HTML link exposed there. Both returned HTTP 200. The
[review record](review.json) binds receipts and separates reported results from
local deductions. Full bodies remain local: the abstract declares arXiv's
nonexclusive distribution license, so redistribution has not been qualified.

The relevant empirical sections attribute most selected-maker profit to
settlement inventory. Full-maker and selected-cohort results differ; neither
is an owned neutral strategy. Full-path regime descriptions lack a demonstrated
decision-time prefix classifier. Last-trade prices are not executable depth,
and one historical winner is not prospective settlement-rule proof. The
collateral comparison assumes segregation, depth normalization and atomic
switches; it cannot replace venue-specific conversion or margin evidence.
Sections 1.1, 3, 4.1, 5.3, 6 and Appendix B were reviewed, not every proof.

Local decision: do not train on profitable-cohort membership, transplant the
regime labels into inference or import a model's quote rent as guaranteed cash.
The final displayed inequality in Lemma 1 also conflicts with positive room
inside its stated screening interval; no runtime gate is imported from it.
This is a methodological caution, not an independent replication or a finding
that all maker strategies lose money.
