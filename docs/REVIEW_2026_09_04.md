## Session-only research corrections

These distinctions apply to this resumed review, not to rewriting past tests or
relaxing execution authority:

1. **Unavailable guarantee:** a source does not prove a positive worst-case
   return. This blocks a guarantee claim, not all expected-value research.
2. **Stress failure:** a candidate loses under specified adverse costs. Report
   the cost scenario and break-even threshold; do not call the hurdle a
   measured fee or conclude that every plausible scenario loses.
3. **Negative expected-value evidence:** a defined decision rule loses after
   measured costs in its scoped causal sample. Include uncertainty, capacity,
   selection history and failed variants. It is not a universal theorem.
4. **Payoff counterexample:** a supposedly guaranteed package pays less than its
   claimed floor in a valid state. This invalidates that guarantee even if the
   state is rare; repair semantics before requesting prices.

Offline exploration may reuse retained public evidence with selection history
explicitly labeled. It does not restore an untouched holdout. A consumed exact
contract remains consumed, and its literal retry trigger remains mandatory.
Any eventual validation needs independent prospective data and after-cost
acceptance rules fixed before access. No private data or protected partial
capture is opened for exploratory convenience.

A displayed midpoint is not necessarily an executable ask or an acquisition
lower bound. An expensive synthetic route does not prove every direct route is
expensive. A cheap route at one instant does not prove fillability or persistence.
Label missing-price and proxy-only rejection results as discovery limitations.

## Economic questions worth answering next

### 1. Binance: integrated carry, not just an entry spread

For quantity-matched **linear**, same-unit long spot / short future exposure,
define basis `B = F - S`, net funding received `H`, and all other net costs `C`.
Before liquidation/default/contract mismatch, the cash-flow identity is:

`profit = q * (B_entry - B_exit) + H - C`.

Thus funding receipts must exceed `C + q * (B_exit - B_entry)` to break even.
A zero entry basis says nothing by itself about that inequality. Conversely,
positive funding does not guarantee profit when exit basis or financing costs
move adversely. Funding notionals vary with mark prices: being delta-matched
does not make funding receipts or capital needs invariant to every market state.

For a dated cash-settled future, account for the actual index settlement and
spot exit difference; do not assume perfect convergence to the owned spot
execution price. Return on total locked spot plus margin/reserve capital is not
the same quantity as basis yield divided by spot purchase notional. Include
collateral interest, borrow, fees, rebalancing, missed settlements, conversion,
liquidation buffer and the feasible alternative use of capital exactly once.

### 2. Polymarket: valid payoff packages plus realistic completion probability

First prove compatible resolution sources, observation intervals, boundaries,
ties and exception states. Then separate a completed package's payout from the
cost of reaching that package. Multi-leg maker execution is not atomic.

For an illustrative two-outcome execution model, let `p` be completion
probability, `G` the profit on completion, `L` the loss on failed completion,
and `c` an additional unconditional expected cost. Then
`EV = p*G - (1-p)*L - c`, so for `G+L > 0`, positive EV needs
`p > (L+c)/(G+L)`. This is an identity, not an estimate of `p` or a market result.
Queue position, conditional adverse selection, partial size, cancellation lag,
hedge depth and timeouts determine those inputs. Public trades do not prove
our hypothetical order would have filled. Rewards are a separate incremental
cash flow and cannot be double-counted with spread or assumed at full pool share.

This suggests research on completion-adjusted package profit and abstention
conditions, not repeated catalog snapshots looking only for taker arbitrage.
Retained-data exploration comes first; no old event or protected capture is
reopened, and no live quoting or incentive manipulation is authorized.

The retained [opposite-lock robustness result](model-research/action-value/polymarket-wallet-opposite-lock-robustness-result-v1-2026-08-31.json)
is a useful concrete example. Its five-tick plus 0.05-pUSD-per-lock scenario
retains 154.9035632142 pUSD aggregate historical locked cash flow, but only
43/66 positive locks, below its frozen 80-percent requirement. That is a failed
robustness gate, not a negative aggregate-cash-flow result. About 97.5 percent
of the stressed aggregate comes from BTC; positive ETH and SOL subtotals do not
establish balanced cross-asset persistence. The source is public-wallet history,
not an owned executable strategy or a justification for acquiring the first leg.
Keep that failure. A distinct prospective investigation could evaluate a
cost-aware abstention rule on independently existing inventory, but must not
filter the observed losing transactions after the fact and relabel the same
sample out-of-sample. No new request or acceptance is authorized here.
