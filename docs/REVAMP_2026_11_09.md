## Working method

Take a deliverable that changes a financial decision or closes a demonstrated
failure, then retain its source/evidence binding. Use existing admissible data
and valid focused checks; do not repeat unchanged panels, daily screens, full
suites or training merely to produce activity. A blocked research family does
not block independent work. The mandatory tool and capture contracts remain
controlling; they do not authorize credentials, accounts, orders, delegation,
provider overwrites, task installation or a broad-search bypass.

Keep runtime and presentation separable. Render unknown and stale evidence
explicitly; observation, simulation, selected backend, actual model residency,
financial qualification and trading authority are different facts. Display no
invented balance, return, win rate or readiness. Keep code-native controls and
charts deterministic. Generated images can explore design; they must not become
fabricated performance or obstruct capital controls.

For unattended visual verification, use the bounded native preview described
in the checkpoint. Earlier cross-process hidden-window experiments returned
black frames. Do not repeat those probes or activate/foreground a window to
work around them. The verified exporter renders the production client and
owner-drawn controls in the UI process, with native control bounds and actual
selection values, then exits within the owning process job. Treat exports as
simulated visual evidence only, not keyboard or live-backend verification.
