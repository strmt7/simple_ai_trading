## Decision order

1. Advance a sound structural/expected-value hypothesis when its exact evidence
   and retry conditions permit a genuinely informative test. Prefer after-cost
   economic value, risk/reward and deployable capacity to prediction accuracy
   alone. Preserve negative results and distinguish missing proof from a theorem
   of impossibility. Do not spend requests repeating a consumed population.
2. Close demonstrated capital-loss paths and execution/recovery gaps. Current
   next boundary: exact terminal-order recovery/rearm and verified legacy scope
   migration; preserve unresolved obligations and route remaining callers through
   the durable authority boundary. Autonomous opening persistence and forward
   venue/product/API-key scope binding are implemented, not permanent account UID;
   independent supervisor/process containment remains core.
3. Improve models, datasets and GPU utilization when causal labels, coverage or
   a measured bottleneck justify it. More rows and more GPU activity alone are
   not improved accuracy or economic value. Preserve backend/precision identity
   and leave unrelated workstation processes untouched.
4. Integrate and verify operator UI, diagnostics and deployment requirements.
   Commercial IP protection belongs here before any protected/paid release,
   not ahead of financial evidence and capital safety. Final whole-tree semantic
   review and exhaustive bug hunting remain mandatory release gates.

Re-rank when evidence, user requirements or a material risk changes. A broken
safety boundary can preempt research that would use it; blocked account access
does not halt unrelated public/offline research or engineering. Choose a bounded
next deliverable with an explicit information gain, failure consequence or
measurable resource benefit. Stop an unproductive branch, not the entire effort.

## Commercial code protection: recorded, not implemented

The user requests resistance to easy source inspection/copying for a possible
future private/paid edition. [Inspected packaging evidence](hardening/commercial-distribution/context.md)
shows that the native launcher still invokes a Python source runtime and the
beta pipeline distributes wheel/sdist artifacts. The current repository is
public and carries an MIT license. Preserve current licensing and releases;
changing visibility cannot recall copies and GitHub retains detached public
forks, as explained in its
[visibility documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/managing-repository-settings/setting-repository-visibility).

Before a protected edition, complete the source-derived hardening proposal and
select the distribution boundary. Evaluate native compilation of proprietary
modules and selective obfuscation of code/constants against a hosted proprietary
strategy/model service with a local deterministic safety gateway. The latter
changes latency, availability, privacy, cost and offline behavior, so it requires
an explicit product decision; no hosting, purchase or account migration now.

A ZIP, wheel, renamed executable or plain bytecode bundle is not an IP boundary.
[PyInstaller documents decompilation of bundled bytecode](https://pyinstaller.org/en/stable/operating-mode.html).
[Nuitka documents separate compilation and constant/data protection](https://nuitka.net/doc/commercial.html);
its claims are not a measured protection result for this app. Any local code or
model remains exposed to sufficiently capable runtime inspection. Encryption
with a client-embedded key must never be presented as secret custody.

Required commercial acceptance work: inventory shipped files and nested archives;
exclude proprietary source/bytecode, source maps, development debug symbols and
research/training assets from the protected artifact; retain required third-party
notices and private matching symbols; verify signed artifacts and updates; test
CLI/native parity and real GPU dependencies without a source checkout. Keep
source readable in development. No obfuscation, license expiry or service outage
may prevent Stop, reconciliation, owned-position reduction or operator diagnostics.
Do not add intrusive anti-debugging, machine fingerprinting or hidden telemetry
without a concrete need and explicit authorization. No unstealable-code promise.

This is a tracked release requirement and initial evidence inventory, not a
completed hardening proposal, packaging implementation, security scan or license
review. Deeper commercial work is queued so it does not displace the first three
priorities. Existing numerical evidence and protected capture rules are unchanged.
